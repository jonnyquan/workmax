# Product Shot Composition Grid

> Five composition archetypes the productShot mode picks from. Each
> drives a different commercial purpose; the agent should match the
> user's `setting` + `lighting` answers from the question_form to
> the most appropriate composition.

## 5 compositions

### 1. Center-stack (PDP main image)
- Product dead center, 60–70% frame fill
- Background: neutral gradient OR solid brand color
- Use case: e-commerce primary listing image

### 2. Rule-of-thirds with breathing room
- Product on left or right vertical third, prop / lifestyle context elsewhere
- 40% negative space
- Use case: editorial product feature, hero banner

### 3. Diagonal energy
- Product on diagonal axis (top-left → bottom-right)
- Implies motion / dynamism
- Use case: launch / new-arrival energy

### 4. Flat-lay top-down
- Camera 90° down, product among supporting items
- 8pt grid alignment of supporting props
- Use case: lifestyle context, "kit" or "set" reveal

### 5. Close-up macro
- Single feature / detail shot (texture, material, mechanism)
- Shallow DOF, dominant focal point
- Use case: secondary PDP image, "why this product" slot

## Lighting × composition pairing

| Lighting | Recommended compositions |
|----------|-------------------------|
| `soft` | 1, 2, 4 |
| `dramatic` | 3, 5 |
| `natural` | 2, 4 |
| `golden_hour` | 2, 3 |

## Anti-patterns

- Product centered AND surrounded by emoji decoration
- Over-stylized props that visually compete with the product
- "Floating" product without contextual grounding (shadow / surface contact)
