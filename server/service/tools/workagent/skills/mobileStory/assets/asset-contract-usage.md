# Creative Asset Contract Usage — Mobile Story

Use `creative_asset_contract.v1` before composing 9:16 story frames, covers, HTML
prototypes, or MP4 story variants.

## Required Reads
- `asset_kind: brand`: logo safe-space, colors, typography, voice, and motion.
- `asset_kind: product`: real product image and visual guidance.
- `asset_kind: character`: identity anchors/reference image for people or mascot.
- `asset_kind: director_style`: optional pacing, lighting, and texture.
- `asset_kind: scene_style`: frame setting, safe zones, crop, and background
  continuity.
- `asset_kind: copy_voice`: hook, captions, CTA, and forbidden claims.
- `asset_kind: motion_rules`: frame pacing, transition language, loop behavior,
  and reduced-motion fallback.

## Story Examples
- Platform safe zones: keep logo/product/CTA out of top and bottom UI overlays.
- Draft brand: show `pending brand confirmation` caveat; do not ship as final.
- Missing product/character assets: ask, skip, or use explicit placeholders.
- Multi-frame story: keep brand/product/character contract stable across frames;
  vary layout and pacing, not identity.
- Copy voice: keep hook/caption/CTA tone consistent across all frames.
- Motion rules: keep transitions aligned to the declared pacing and preserve
  static legibility of logo/product/CTA.
